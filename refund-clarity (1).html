Researcher notes · not participant instructions

A hypothesis, not a validated solution.

What this version tests

Can applicants explain their refusal refund and distinguish payment from submission in this proposed flow? This prototype alone cannot show an improvement over the existing product.

The consequential assumption

Payment holds the application until the applicant explicitly submits it. Pre-payment verification, the paid hold and a full refund before submission all require operational validation.

Abandonment risk: someone may pay, assume they are finished and leave without submitting. Observe whether they recognise the outstanding action. A live system would need recovery, reminders and an agreed hold-expiry policy; none is established here.

How to run the test

Preset an outcome, enter participant mode and read only the neutral task. Observe the first action before asking the questions below. Do not reveal answers or coach. Test the paid state for mistaken completion.

  1. Is refund protection confirmed for this application?
  2. On authority refusal, what is refunded and what is lost? Which conditions matter?
  3. What does the next action commit you to?
  4. Has the application been sent to the authority?
  5. What would cancellation mean at this moment?

Record correct, incorrect or uncertain responses, assistance needed, first action and mistaken completion. Recognising pending protection as unknown is correct. No participant responses are recorded by this HTML.

Reveal expected answers for the current state

What would establish improvement?

Compare clearer disclosure against an appropriate baseline while holding the payment model constant. Evaluate a separate submission step independently if operationally feasible. No usability results have been collected.

Illustrative policy and scope

This is an independent exploration, not an audit of Atlys or its actual policy. Prices, residency rules and refund terms are fictional. This check concerns refund protection, not visa eligibility.

Sample policy: verified UK residency qualifies for protection. After submission, authority refusal returns government and service fees (₹10,600); courier ₹600 is excluded. Without protection, refusal returns ₹0. False documents and withdrawal after submission are excluded. Before submission, cancellation returns the entire ₹11,200. Actual withdrawal options after submission are outside this prototype.

No real payment, document verification or government submission occurs. State resets on reload; a production service would need durable application state and recovery.
